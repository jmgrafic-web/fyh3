About Begur

The Crown Jewel of the Costa Brava

Begur occupies a unique position — literally and figuratively. Perched on a hill crowned by a 16th-century castle, it surveys six spectacular coves below, each with crystalline water and pine-scented cliffs.

Unlike many coastal towns that have surrendered their character to mass tourism, Begur has retained its Catalan soul. The medieval centre remains beautifully intact — stone streets, independent restaurants, a local culture that doesn't evaporate in September.

This combination — authentic village life, exceptional natural beauty, and strong investment fundamentals — makes Begur one of the most compelling addresses on the Mediterranean. International buyers have understood this for decades, and demand consistently outstrips supply.

The Coves

Six Perfect Coves

Each cove has its own character. Understanding which suits your lifestyle helps determine where to focus your search.

Sa Riera

The largest and most accessible beach. Sandy, sheltered, family-oriented. Direct road access and summer facilities. Most popular with locals and visitors alike.

Aiguablava

Postcard perfect. Crystal turquoise water, dramatic cliffs, pine forest to the shore. Home to the iconic Parador hotel. Properties here are exceptionally rare and valuable.

Fornells

A quiet bay with a small marina. More residential and authentic than the other coves. Excellent for sailing. A favourite with returning visitors who've discovered its particular magic.

Aiguafreda

Small and intimate, surrounded by rock and pine. One of the hidden gems of the Costa Brava. Very limited development — properties here are extremely rare.

Sa Tuna

An old fishing cove with its original character largely intact. Small beach, traditional houses in faded Mediterranean colours. Deeply charming and increasingly sought after.

Platja Fonda

The most secluded — accessible only on foot. Wild, unspoiled, no facilities. For those who want a genuinely private Mediterranean experience.

Property Market 2025

What Your Budget Unlocks

Begur's market is structurally tight. Supply is limited by geography and strict planning laws. Here's a realistic overview.

Village Apartment

€180k – €380k

Converted stone apartments in the medieval centre. Often with terraces and castle views. Good summer rental income potential.

Village House

€350k – €750k

Traditional Catalan stone houses requiring varying degrees of renovation. Character impossible to recreate — the renovation challenge is often the opportunity.

Modern Villa

€600k – €1.8M

Contemporary or recently renovated villas with pool, sea views, and architectural quality. Most sought-after segment among international buyers.

Cove-Front Property

€1.2M – €4M+

Exceptionally rare. Direct cove access or first-line position. These properties rarely appear on the open market and command significant premiums.

Practical Guide

Living in Begur

Getting there

Girona Airport: 45 min. Barcelona El Prat: 2h. No direct train — a car is essential for living here comfortably.

International schools

Closest international schools in Girona (30 min). Most foreign buyers use Begur as a second home — full-time schooling is rarely the primary concern.

Healthcare

Local medical centre in Begur village. Hospital in Palamós (20 min). Barcelona's private hospitals 1h45 away.

Best season

Peak July–August. Shoulder May–June, September–October — ideal conditions, fewer crowds. Winter is quiet but increasingly popular with remote workers.

Rental potential

Strong demand, very limited supply. Tourist licences are restricted — apply early. A well-positioned villa can generate €40,000–€80,000 per summer season.

Nearby

Palafrugell market (10 min). Girona dining and culture (45 min). Barcelona (2h). Figueres and the Dalí Museum (1h).
