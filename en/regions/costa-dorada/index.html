Costa Dorada

The region

The golden south

The Costa Dorada stretches south of Barcelona along the Tarragona coastline — wide golden beaches, Roman amphitheatres, and Catalonia's most affordable coastal property market. This is where value-conscious buyers can still find houses that need restoring at prices that allow for ambitious renovation projects.

Sitges anchors the northern end — cosmopolitan, architecturally rich, and increasingly international. Further south, Altafulla and Torredembarra offer authentic Catalan life, good beaches, and an untouched historic core. Tarragona city brings UNESCO heritage, a thriving food scene, and a pace of life that feels genuinely Mediterranean.

Key towns

Where to look

Sitges

The jewel of the Costa Dorada. Modernista villas, a cosmopolitan arts scene, and a beautiful historic centre by the sea.

Altafulla

An intact medieval village behind its own castle. Stone houses, narrow lanes, and a wonderful beach below. Still a discovery.

Tarragona

Roman capital of Hispania. A living UNESCO heritage site with a thriving food scene and undervalued property market.

Cambrils

One of Catalonia's finest food destinations. A genuine working harbour and excellent beaches. Growing quietly in prestige.

Torredembarra

Family-friendly town with wide beaches, a 17th-century castle, and authentic Catalan character. Excellent value.

Salou

Long sandy beaches with good infrastructure. Suited to rental investment or families seeking a lively coastal base.
