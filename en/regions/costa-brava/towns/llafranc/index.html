About Llafranc

The Most Complete Village on This Stretch of Coast

Llafranc offers what most Costa Brava villages cannot: a proper sandy beach long enough to breathe, a working promenade with excellent restaurants, and a natural setting that rivals anything on the Mediterranean.

The lighthouse at Cap de Sant Sebastià marks the headland between Llafranc and Calella de Palafrugell. The walk between the two villages — 40 minutes along the cliff — is one of the great short walks of the Costa Brava. It gives a sense of the scale and wildness of this coastline that no photograph quite captures.

Llafranc is connected to Tamariu by a coastal path (10 minutes) and to Palafrugell by road (5 minutes). It's the most practically convenient of the three Palafrugell coves — which helps explain why its property market is the most active and liquid of the three.

The Triangle

Llafranc, Tamariu & Calella — Three Villages, One Choice

The three Palafrugell coves are connected on foot and each has a distinct character. Understanding the differences helps narrow your search.

Llafranc

The most active. Sandy beach, good restaurants, lighthouse walk. Best for families and those who want genuine amenities. Strongest rental market of the three.

Tamariu

The quietest. Smaller beach, more intimate, fewer visitors. For buyers who want absolute peace and don't mind limited local services.

Calella

The most characterful. Traditional fishing village with a famous flower festival. Rocky beach, flower-draped houses, artisan market. Very strong community feel.

Property Market 2025

What to Expect in Llafranc

The most liquid of the Palafrugell coves, with a wider range of property types and better rental infrastructure than its neighbours.

Seafront Apartment

€280k – €600k

Apartments on or near the promenade. Direct sea views command a significant premium. Strong tourist rental potential — these are the most in-demand units in the market.

Village House

€380k – €750k

Traditional houses in the village fabric. Often 2–3 storeys, with terraces and good renovation potential. Walking distance to beach and restaurants.

Hillside Villa

€550k – €1.6M

Villas on the pine hillside above the cove. Pool, garden, bay views. The premium end of the market — architectural quality varies widely, making expert assessment essential.

Lighthouse Area

€900k – €2.5M

Exceptional properties near Cap de Sant Sebastià with panoramic views. The most prestigious location between Tamariu and Calella. Extremely rare supply.
