The region

Where the Pyrenees
meet the sea.

The Costa Brava stretches 214km along the northeastern corner of Catalonia, from Blanes in the south to the French border. It is, by any measure, the most architecturally and naturally spectacular coast in Spain.

Rugged limestone cliffs drop to hidden coves of crystalline water. Behind them, dense cork forests give way to medieval hilltop villages. The light here — particularly in spring and autumn — is painterly.

International buyers discover that the Costa Brava offers something genuinely rare: authentic architecture, a low-key local culture that hasn't been erased by tourism, and access to some of the most remarkable natural landscape in Europe.

Life here

More than a
summer destination

The Costa Brava has outgrown its image as a package-holiday coast. Today, it is a year-round destination for those who value authenticity, nature, quality food and a pace of life that is no longer available in most of Europe.

Architecture & design

A region with deep respect for craft. From restored masies to contemporary concrete villas, the quality of building here is exceptional.

Year-round lifestyle

Mild winters, warm springs and long autumns mean your second home works for you 9–10 months of the year — not just July and August.

Exceptional food & wine

Home to El Celler de Can Roca, multiple Michelin stars and a market-cooking tradition that makes every meal remarkable.

International connections

Girona Airport (40 min) connects directly to Amsterdam, Brussels, London, Paris and over 30 European cities year-round.

The market

What to expect
when buying here

Price ranges

Entry-level properties in well-located towns start from €350,000 for apartments needing renovation. Character houses with potential range from €500,000 to €1.2M. Exceptional properties with sea views, established gardens and high-quality finishes: €1.5M–€5M+.

The Costa Brava has consistently outperformed other Spanish coastal markets over the past decade, driven by limited buildable land and sustained international demand.

Renovation potential

The greatest value opportunity on the Costa Brava lies in properties that need architectural transformation. Many buyers purchase for €600,000–€900,000 and invest a further €150,000–€300,000 in renovation to create homes worth €1.2M–€2M.

Our architectural team identifies this potential — the structural bones, the views, the planning flexibility — before you commit to anything.
