Where to Buy

Three Coastlines.
One Expert Team.

The Catalan Coast stretches from the wild cliffs of the Costa Brava down through the Barcelona Maresme to the sun-drenched Costa Dorada. Each region has its own character, its own market, and its own particular appeal. We know all three intimately.

North Catalonia

Costa Brava

Wild cliffs, hidden coves, medieval villages perched above turquoise water. The Costa Brava is the most dramatic and most sought-after coastline in Catalonia — and possibly the entire Mediterranean.

Strict planning protections have kept it from the overdevelopment that defines much of the Spanish coast. The result: a structurally undersupplied market where values have risen consistently and the lifestyle offer is genuinely irreplaceable.

Barcelona Coast

Costa Barcelona — Maresme

The coast that runs north from Barcelona along the Maresme offers something unique on the Mediterranean: genuine coastal living within 30–40 minutes of one of Europe's great cities by train.

Sitges, Premià de Mar, Caldes d'Estrac — towns with real year-round communities, international schools nearby, and a property market driven by Barcelona professionals who want to live by the sea without sacrificing urban access.

South Catalonia

Costa Dorada

The Golden Coast stretches south from Barcelona towards the Ebro Delta. Long sandy beaches, a warmer microclimate than the Costa Brava, and property prices that still offer genuine value compared to the north.

Tarragona — a UNESCO World Heritage Roman city — sits at its heart. The region suits buyers who want more beach, more sun, and more space for their budget, without sacrificing the Catalan quality of life.
