What we do

From search to settled.
We guide you all the way.

Discovery

Property Search

We search the open market, off-market networks and private contacts to find properties that match your vision — not just your budget.

Due Diligence

Architectural Assessment

Before you sign anything, we inspect every wall, roof, drain, and planning permit. Our detailed report tells you exactly what you're buying.

Representation

Buyer's Agent

We represent you, not the seller. We negotiate, coordinate the legal team, manage the notary process, and ensure a clean purchase.

Transformation

Architecture & Renovation

From concept to completion, our in-house studio designs and manages every renovation — from a single bathroom to a complete structural transformation.

Interiors

Interior Design

Homes that feel lived-in and considered. Mediterranean light, local materials, global elegance. Curated down to the last detail.

After Purchase

Property Management

Your home, looked after. Key holding, maintenance, supplier management, pre-arrival preparation. A second home should be effortless.

The process

How it works

A clear, guided journey from first conversation to moving in.

1

Step one

Tell us your dream

We start with a deep conversation — your lifestyle, your vision, your family's needs, your budget. Not just a property brief, but a life brief.

2

Step two

We search and shortlist

We go to market — open listings, private networks, direct outreach to owners. We assess each property architecturally before presenting it to you.

3

Step three

We assess before you buy

Our pre-purchase report covers structure, services, planning history, renovation potential and realistic costs. You know exactly what you're buying.

4

Step four

We transform your house

If renovation is needed, our studio takes it from sketch to handover. Architecture, interiors, project management — all under one roof.

5

Step five

Enjoy your Mediterranean life

We continue as your local partner — property management, maintenance, concierge services. Your second home, made effortless.
