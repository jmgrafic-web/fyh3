Costa Barcelona–Maresme

The region

Where Barcelona meets the Mediterranean

The Maresme coast stretches 48 kilometres north of Barcelona — wine villages, fishing towns and sheltered beaches where the Catalan Mountains meet the sea. A 30-minute train from the city, with its own gentler microclimate.

International buyers here get rare combination: genuine Mediterranean life, proximity to one of Europe's finest cities, excellent schools, and a still-accessible property market. Our architects know every street, municipality, and planning regulation across the region.

Key towns

Where to look

Alella

Hilltop wine village with ancient cellars and stone farmhouses. Panoramic views and deep quiet.

Cabrils

Elevated village with sweeping sea views. Good schools, strong international community.

Sant Andreu de Llavaneres

The Maresme's most sought-after address. Modernist villas, private marina, discreet elegance.

Arenys de Mar

Authentic fishing town with a thriving food scene and historic centre to renovate.

Premià de Dalt

Hillside village with Modernista architecture gems waiting to be restored.

Teià

Peaceful village on the natural park boundary. Popular with architects and creative professionals.
