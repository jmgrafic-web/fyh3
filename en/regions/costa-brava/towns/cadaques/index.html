About Cadaqués

Deliberately Remote. Exceptionally Beautiful.

Cadaqués sits at the end of a serpentine mountain road on the edge of the Cap de Creus Natural Park — the most easterly point of the Iberian Peninsula. That road is not a flaw. It's the filter that has preserved everything you see.

Whitewashed houses cascade down rocky hillsides to a series of small pebble beaches. A 16th-century church watches over the bay. In the evenings, the light does something extraordinary — the kind of light that drove Dalí to build his house here, and that kept Duchamp, Picasso, Man Ray, and García Lorca returning every summer for decades.

Cadaqués is not for everyone. There are no big supermarkets, no golf courses, no resort infrastructure. It's for people who want something different — authenticity, natural beauty, and a very particular atmosphere that is genuinely irreplaceable.

Cultural Legacy

The Artists Who Came and Never Really Left

Cadaqués attracted the most significant artists of the 20th century. That legacy — visible in its architecture, culture, and the type of person it attracts today — is part of what you're buying.

Salvador Dalí
Spent summers here his entire life. Built his home in Portlligat, steps from Cadaqués.
Marcel Duchamp
Spent 20 consecutive summers in Cadaqués. Wrote he felt more at home here than anywhere.
Pablo Picasso
Visited in 1910, a pivotal year. The landscape influenced his development of Cubism.
Man Ray
Photographed Cadaqués extensively. His images defined its visual identity for a generation.
Property Market 2025

The Cadaqués Market

Supply is extraordinarily limited. No new construction is permitted within the protected zones — which is most of the village and coastline. Prices reflect both scarcity and irreplaceability.

Village Apartment

€250k – €500k

Whitewashed village apartments, often on narrow streets with sea glimpses. The entry point to owning in Cadaqués — and already exceptional.

Village House

€500k – €1.2M

Traditional fisherman's houses and townhouses, often requiring renovation. Unique character — the kind of spaces you simply cannot find elsewhere.

Villa with Views

€900k – €2.5M

Larger properties on the hillside with bay and Cap de Creus views. These are landmark addresses. Rarely available, rapidly absorbed.

Cap de Creus Estate

€1.5M – €5M+

Exceptional rural properties in the natural park surroundings. Subject to strict planning limitations but offer unparalleled privacy and landscape.

Important: Cadaqués sits largely within protected zones (Cap de Creus Natural Park and urban heritage protection). Planning restrictions are significant and strictly enforced. Any property with renovation intent must be assessed by an architect before purchase. We cannot overstate this.

What to Know

Before You Fall in Love with Cadaqués

It will change your relationship with time

There is no Amazon delivery, no 24h supermarket, no fast food. The pace is Mediterranean in the truest sense. Buyers who embrace this find it transformative. Those who resist it find it frustrating.

The road is part of the experience

The mountain road from Roses takes 20 minutes of concentrated driving. In summer, you share it with visitors. The result: Cadaqués feels worlds away from everywhere. This is by design.

Renovation rules are strict

Heritage protection means you cannot simply demolish and rebuild. Work must respect the original character. This limits what can be done — but also protects the value of what exists.

It attracts a very particular buyer

Artists, writers, architects, collectors, high-profile creatives and intellectuals. The community that gathers here every summer is unlike any other on the coast — and is itself a significant part of the value proposition.
