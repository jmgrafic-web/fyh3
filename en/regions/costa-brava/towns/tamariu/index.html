About Tamariu

The Cove That Kept Its Secret

Tamariu is what many Costa Brava villages were before the tourist boom — a quiet fishing cove, pine trees to the water's edge, a handful of restaurants, and an unhurried pace that defines the best of Mediterranean life.

The village sits in a protected bay, flanked by rocky headlands. The beach is small and perfectly formed. The water quality is consistently excellent — awarded Blue Flag status year after year. Development beyond the original village footprint is not permitted.

Those who own here tend not to sell. The market is thin — a handful of transactions per year — but that scarcity is precisely what preserves its character and underpins values long-term.

The Lifestyle

A Day in Tamariu

Morning

Coffee at the terrace of Can Rafa. Swim before 9am when the cove is yours. Walk the coastal path to Llafranc — 45 minutes through pine and rock.

Afternoon

Lunch at one of the beach restaurants. Kayak to the hidden coves only accessible from the water. Return in the late afternoon when the light is golden and low.

Evening

Aperitivo on the terrace as the sun drops behind the headland. Drive to Palafrugell or Begur for dinner. Return to absolute quiet.

Weekend

Girona for Saturday market and restaurants. Drive the coast road through Begur and Calella. Dinner with friends at a private villa. This is the life people are buying.

Property Market 2025

The Tamariu Market

Properties in Tamariu almost never reach the public market. Most transactions happen through local networks. This is why working with an agent embedded in the community matters.

Cove-Side Apartment

€300k – €600k

Apartments within walking distance of the beach. Often older properties with renovation potential. Sea views command a premium.

Traditional House

€450k – €850k

Fisherman's houses and traditional village homes. Character and location that cannot be replicated. Often require architectural renovation.

Hillside Villa

€550k – €1.5M

Villas on the pine-covered hillside with bay views. The premium category — pool, garden, privacy, and one of the most beautiful outlooks on the Costa Brava.

The off-market reality: In Tamariu, the best properties are rarely listed. Owners are reluctant to sell and often prefer to find buyers privately.
